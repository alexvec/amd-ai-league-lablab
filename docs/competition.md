## 4. Eligibility

- Individual participation only; teams not allowed. One account per person; self-referrals and fake accounts removed along with their points. Source: MAIN.
- Joining the lablab Discord (discord.gg/lablabai) is required for Match participation. Source: MAIN.
- Age 18+; not located in a country or territory under US economic or trade sanctions; not on a prohibited-persons list; not previously banned from lablab. Source: TERMS Sections 3A and 11.
- Organizers may participate but are not prize-eligible. Source: RULES. Teams with lablab staff excluded from judging and awards. Source: GUIDE.
- Other geographic limits: none found on MAIN. Event-specific official rules: UNVERIFIED (no separate rules document found).
- Note: GUIDE says everyone must be a member of a team on lablab.ai, "This applies for solo participants as well" (a solo team of one). LIVE refers to "teams" and "2,600 teams". This is lablab platform mechanics, not a contradiction of the individual-only rule, but a solo team is presumably what the platform creates. Exact handling: UNVERIFIED.
- Joining requires an AMD Developer Program SSO sign-in (MAIN join link: lablab.ai/sso?callbackUrl=... enroll=true). Whether a separate lablab account is needed: UNVERIFIED but likely.

### Generic lablab submission form (applies by default; event form takes priority)
Source: DELIVER (https://lablab.ai/delivering-your-hackathon-solution) and RULES (https://lablab.ai/hackathon-rules). Both say the form and event page take priority and events can add, remove, or make fields optional. Whether this AMD League uses these fields for Matches 4-6: UNVERIFIED.
- Title 5-50 chars; short description 50-255 chars; long description 600-2,000 chars; categories and technologies.
- Cover image optional, 16:9.
- Video MP4 or MOV, up to 300 MB, recommended under 5 minutes.
- Slides as PDF.
- GitHub repository, must be public.
- Demo application platform and demo application URL (RULES: URL required for interactive evaluation).
- Submit before the event deadline; submissions can be edited until then. RULES: five-minute grace period.
- GUIDE: "you will need to deliver a working prototype of your project that others will be able to use online", plus video and pitch deck.
